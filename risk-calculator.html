Acknowledgement

          

          

            
This tool estimates the 2-week risk of treatment-requiring ROP from gestational age, postmenstrual age, and P-score (a measure of vascular severity).
            It should be used to assist in determining whether a 1-week vs 2-week follow-up exam is necessary.
            Its aim is to support, not replace, clinical judgment and is for investigational use only.
            It has only been validated for use on US populations.

          

          

            
          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        

          

            
            
When to Use

          

          

            
This tool estimates the 2-week probability of treatment-requiring ROP development. Use it for risk-based scheduling and escalation discussions within the next 14 days.

            
    
              
  • Inputs: Gestational age (GA), current postmenstrual age (PMA), and P-score.
    • 
              
  • Input ranges: GA 22–31 wks; PMA 30–50 wks; P-score 1–9.
    • 
              
  • Outputs: Risk of treatment within the next two weeks: Low Risk or High Risk.
    • 
            

          

          

            
          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        

          

            
            
Considerations

          

          

            
    
              
  • Extrapolation: Avoid use outside the input ranges.
    • 
              
  • P-score variability: Inter-rater differences can shift risk.
    • 
              
  • Population differences: Consider local validation/calibration.
    • 
              
  • Time window: Estimate applies to the next 14 days only.
    • 
            

          

          

            
          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        

          

            
            
Why Use

          

          

            
    
              
  • Objective: Quantifies 2-week TR-ROP risk to complement exam findings.
    • 
              
  • Triage: Supports risk-based follow-up scheduling.
    • 
              
  • Communication: Clear categories for team/family discussions.
    • 
              
  • Quality: Transparent inputs/outputs enable audit and calibration.
